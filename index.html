The area & the development

01 · The area — Costa Brava

Between the Mediterranean and the hills of Baix Empordà, Calonge brings together the best of the Costa Brava: crystal-clear beaches, top gastronomy and unspoiled nature just minutes away.

10 min from Sant Antoni de Calonge beaches
45 min from Girona Airport
1h 20 min from Barcelona
Direct motorway access via AP-7
Golf, marina and shops within 15 min
02 · The development — Vescomptat de Cabanyes

Surrounded by pine forests just minutes from the Mediterranean, Vescomptat de Cabanyes is a fully completed development, formally approved by the local council. Its 5,000-8,000 m² plots were designed for those seeking space, privacy and a preserved natural setting. A single house already built shows the architectural potential of the area.
